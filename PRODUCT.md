# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Travelers from anywhere in the world looking to book quality accommodation in Serbia. Accommodation owners in Serbia who want to become hosts and receive bookings through the platform.

## Product Purpose

BGApartman is an accommodation booking platform focused on Serbia. It helps guests discover and book stays, and gives accommodation owners a path to become hosts and list their places. Its promise is to offer the best quality accommodations at the best possible prices in Serbia.

## Brand Personality

Trustworthy, welcoming, quality-focused.

## Anti-references

No specific reference brands or anti-reference sites were given. Prefer industry-standard booking and marketplace conventions. Avoid changing visual treatments that are already designed without an explicit request, and avoid novel interaction patterns that make searching, booking, or hosting harder to understand.

## Design Principles

- Make booking clear and dependable: help guests understand accommodation details, prices, availability, and booking terms before committing.
- Keep quality and value visible together so guests can compare stays with confidence.
- Support both sides of the marketplace, including the path from guest to host and the tools hosts need to list accommodations.
- Use familiar, consistent marketplace interactions. Preserve established visual design unless the task explicitly calls for a change.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Support keyboard and screen-reader navigation, adequate contrast and touch targets, reduced-motion preferences, and clear language for travelers from different countries.
